About the Department

Department overview

Chemical engineering focuses on the design, operation and improvement of processes that convert raw materials into valuable products. Chemical engineers work across chemicals, energy, food, pharmaceuticals, water treatment and materials.

  • Process safety and environment

Vision

Support capable, ethical and sustainability-minded engineers who contribute through process knowledge and lifelong learning.

Mission

Build strong fundamentals, encourage practical inquiry, teamwork and safety-conscious problem solving.

Integrity, curiosity, collaboration, evidence-based decisions and respect for people and the environment.
